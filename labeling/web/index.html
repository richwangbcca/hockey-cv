Can this frame support rink positions?

Usable: the camera shows enough rink context to place at least some visible on-ice people in rink coordinates.

Unusable: a close-up, bench, crowd, ad, transition, or ice view without enough rink context.

Choose a visible landmark

Start with faceoff dots and clear line/board intersections. Choose a named point below, then click its center in the image. Mark only what you can see.

Optional: lines, arcs, and areas to ignore

A line or arc is an open trace along visible paint. An ignore area is a closed boundary around a graphic or bench region. These are optional; the rink fit currently uses named points.

Unseen points have no coordinates. You can usually just leave them unmarked.

Does the rink fit the image?

Use at least four named points to fit the rink. Optionally reserve an extra point for checking: it measures error without influencing the fit. Four suitable fit points plus visual inspection are enough to accept.
